# How to take all measurements

Your costume is made to your body, so accurate measurements matter. Below you will find the list of measurements we need and illustrated instructions for each of them.

📥 **Download the measurement form:**
- [**PDF with fillable form and instructions**](https://github.com/etereshop-sales/How-to-take-all-measurements/raw/main/downloads/How-to-take-measurements-EtereShop.pdf) (recommended)
- [DOCX](https://github.com/etereshop-sales/How-to-take-all-measurements/raw/main/downloads/measurements-form.docx) · [XLSX](https://github.com/etereshop-sales/How-to-take-all-measurements/raw/main/downloads/measurements-form.xlsx) · [TXT](https://github.com/etereshop-sales/How-to-take-all-measurements/raw/main/downloads/measurements-form.txt)

Fill in the form and send it back to us by e-mail or WhatsApp. Please tell us whether you measured in centimetres or inches.

## Before you start

- **Wear thin clothing** — a T-shirt and leggings, or underwear. No jackets or thick jumpers.
- **Ask someone to help** — it is hard to measure yourself accurately, especially shoulders and back.
- **Keep the tape snug** — flat against the body, not tight. Stand straight and relaxed.
